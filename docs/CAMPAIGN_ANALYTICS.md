# Campaign analytics

Counts come from campaign, audience, draft, approval, execution, and enrollment rows. The summary can be limited by campaign, owner, and a created-at range. It includes campaign counts by lifecycle, audience size, eligible preparation, suppressed, human-owned, unknown eligibility, qualified leads in the audience, drafts generated, drafts awaiting approval, approved recipient drafts, simulation steps, failed internal tasks, stopped enrollments, and average preparation duration when step timestamps exist.

`external.sent`, `external.delivered`, `external.replies`, `external.meetings`, and `external.conversions` are the string `NOT_AVAILABLE`. The screen prints that string. The API does not calculate open rate, reply rate, click rate, conversion rate, or revenue.
