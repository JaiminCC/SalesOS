# WhatsApp consent policy

An ordinary inbound message records purpose `INBOUND_INTERACTION` with status `UNKNOWN`. It is not promotional consent. Promotional consent is not copied from one business phone to another.

Exact commands `stop`, `unsubscribe`, `opt out`, `opt-out`, `stopall`, and `cancel` record promotional `OPT_OUT` and a WhatsApp suppression row. A longer sentence that only mentions stopping is `OPT_OUT_REVIEW` and stays for a person. AI output cannot clear suppression.

A blocked draft cannot be approved. Preparation after opt-out stores a blocked draft and does not create a provider attempt.
